About

Lastborn Creative is built around two things: business and product ad videos, and short-form content for creators and brands. Every edit starts with the same question: what makes someone stop scrolling in the first three seconds.

Whether it's a product ad with a clear problem and payoff, or short-form content built to grow a following, the goal is the same: capture attention fast, hold it, and give the viewer a reason to act, whether that's buying, following, or reaching out.

What guides the work is pacing, retention, and a clear call to action, the details that turn a watched video into a sale or a new follower, not just a finished file.
