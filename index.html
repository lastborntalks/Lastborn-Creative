About

Lastborn Creative is a video editing practice built on one belief: raw footage is only half a story. The other half is shaped in the edit, through pacing, rhythm, sound, and the small decisions that decide whether someone keeps watching or scrolls away.

My goal with every project is to transform ordinary clips into polished, engaging content that captures attention and communicates the client's message clearly and effectively, whether that's a sermon, a product launch, a highlight reel, or a full YouTube documentary.

What guides the work is consistency, attention to detail, and a genuine commitment to delivering videos people actually enjoy watching, not just videos that get finished.
