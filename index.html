About

Story Before Effects

Lastborn Creative is a video editing practice built around one idea: the edit is where a story actually gets made. Raw footage is only half the work, pacing, sound, and structure are the other half.

My range spans product and business ads, short-form content built for retention, narrative storytelling, documentary-style coverage, and horror-style short-form, adapting the craft to whatever the footage and the client actually need.
